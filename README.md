## How to read it

This is an **opinionated reference-stack research snapshot**, not a neutral ranking, live deployment inventory, or automatic installation recommendation. Fit scores depend on the documented workload and stack lens. The deep research cards and lightweight harness catalog have distinct rubrics; their scores are not interchangeable.

Recorded review dates, license observations, confidence, limitations, and historical usage statements retain their original scope. A score or dated observation does not prove current upstream behavior, present adoption, or measured effectiveness. Recheck high-impact recommendations before acting on them.

The repository-quality standards are opinionated technical audit profiles, not universal requirements. They target private-repository engineering readiness; they do not establish publication clearance.

## Source and maintenance

The selected Markdown is maintained in a separate canonical source and copied here at the same relative paths without export-time rewriting. This repository is a derived reading surface, not a second editing authority. Changes should be made and reviewed at the canonical owner before a fresh projection.

Only the selected research folders and four repository-quality standards are included. Source history, private operating records, policy files, certification ledgers, and detailed review receipts are excluded. A projection check does not authorize changing repository visibility.
