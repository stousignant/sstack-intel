# AI Model Capability Frontiers

> Reference-stack lens: [AI engineering](index.md).

## Working synthesis

The durable lesson from the legacy AI frontier notes is not the timeline panic. It is the shape of capability improvement: multimodal world information, reliability, synthetic/self-generated tasks, closed-loop experimentation, and private-domain augmentation.

## Frontiers to track

- Multimodality is useful not only for input/output UX but because images, video, and audio provide world information that text captures incompletely. Vision especially improves reasoning over diagrams, spatial structure, physics, and visual communication.
- Reliability improvements may matter more than raw capability jumps: useful models should ask clarifying questions, admit missing information, and omit irrelevant detail rather than confidently filling gaps.
- Synthetic data/self-generated tasks are plausible capability levers when models can create adversarial examples, imagined problems, or experiment loops, but should be evaluated by external benchmarks and real transfer, not hype.
- Self-improving AI research systems are best understood as closed-loop research pipelines: mine existing knowledge, propose variants, run experiments, analyze results, and allocate more compute to promising branches.
- For practical personal infrastructure, prefer intelligence augmentation on private data over chasing generalized AGI. Smaller/open/private models can already transform workflows when connected to local knowledge and tools.

## What to discard

- Fixed “everything changes in X years” predictions.
- ASI framing without primary-source verification.
- Immortality/singularity/telepathy/simulation-theory claims unless a future research page explicitly investigates them.
