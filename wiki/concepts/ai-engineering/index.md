# AI Engineering Concepts

This finite concept collection links 21 complementary synthesis pages. It uses an opinionated reference-stack lens rather than aiming for universal tutorial coverage or declaring installed infrastructure. The lens favors Hermes as cockpit, human-readable Markdown as durable knowledge, GBrain as its derived retrieval layer, GitHub as work/evidence truth, small maintained skills, deterministic checks, least privilege, independent review, and bounded experiments before custom orchestration.

Recommendations describe fit under that lens, not universal product quality. Original source dates, confidence, caveats and review scope remain on the pages; reorganization is not fresh research or rescoring. Public sources and explicitly selected intelligence provide evidence without requiring private project access. Private operating state, goals, issue provenance and local experiment details remain outside this collection. Inclusion here is not export certification, adoption or publication authority.

## Boundaries

Keep the coding-system thesis, lifecycle, harness, loops, parallel execution and work-packet contracts separate: they answer different questions. Likewise memory principles, layer ownership and graph-vs-RAG trade-offs are complementary, not duplicate pages. Research preprints and project-reported results retain their source caveats; a good-looking graph or completed agent run is not proof of reliable work.
