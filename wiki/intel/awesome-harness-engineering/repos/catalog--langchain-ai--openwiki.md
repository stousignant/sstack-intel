# langchain-ai/openwiki
## Quick take
A self-maintaining Markdown wiki generator with durable page jobs and source-versioned claims for agent-readable knowledge.
## Score
**87/100**
- **Harness relevance — 23/30:** Builds and maintains agent-readable documentation as a durable codebase or personal-memory layer.
- **Practical pattern value — 24/25:** Grounded claims, evidence versions, durable page jobs, no-op updates, and finalization checks are directly transferable.
- **Evidence and maturity — 18/20:** The active LangChain MIT project has extensive docs, multiple providers, connectors, coding-agent integrations, and CI recipes.
- **Novelty — 15/15:** Proposition-level evidence tracking with deterministic retention and retraction is unusually differentiated.
- **Risk-adjusted accessibility — 7/10:** The CLI and local outputs are approachable, but generation costs, credentials, connectors, and default telemetry add risk.
## Why it matters
- Tracks material propositions back to exact evidence and rechecks them before deciding an update is a no-op.
- Persists each page, claims sidecar, verification, and source checkpoint before advancing an interruptible run.
## Caveat
Repository-grounded claims do not cover connector-derived facts, and generated documentation still depends on model behavior and configured credentials.
