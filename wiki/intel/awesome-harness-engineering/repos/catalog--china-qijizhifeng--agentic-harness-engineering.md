# china-qijizhifeng/agentic-harness-engineering
## Quick take
AHE is a research-oriented system for automatically evolving coding-agent harness components from rollout evidence.
## Score
**85/100**
- **Harness relevance — 30/30:** It directly evaluates and evolves prompts, tools, middleware, skills, subagents, and memory around a fixed model.
- **Practical pattern value — 20/25:** Trace-backed evaluate-analyze-improve loops offer reusable methods, though the implementation is operationally heavy.
- **Evidence and maturity — 17/20:** Code, detailed methodology, a paper, and reported cross-benchmark experiments provide strong documented evidence.
- **Novelty — 15/15:** Observability-driven automatic evolution of multiple harness components is unusually differentiated.
- **Risk-adjusted accessibility — 3/10:** MIT licensing helps, but E2B templates, high concurrency, large traces, and model costs make evaluation heavy.
## Why it matters
- Makes component, experience, and decision observability explicit across iterative harness changes.
- Offers a concrete evaluate-analyze-improve loop with auditable edits and reported transfer experiments.
## Caveat
Benchmark results are repository claims, and reproducing them requires substantial sandbox, model, trace, and concurrency resources.
