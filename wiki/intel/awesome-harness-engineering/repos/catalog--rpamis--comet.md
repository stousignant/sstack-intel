A resumable coding-workflow and skill platform spanning requirements, multi-agent delivery, acceptance, memory, and evaluation.

- **Practical pattern value — 25/25:** Native and phased workflows, deterministic routing, recoverable archives, dependency-aware worktrees, and rubric-based skill evaluation are directly transferable.
