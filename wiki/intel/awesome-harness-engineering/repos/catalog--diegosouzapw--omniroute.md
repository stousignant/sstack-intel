# diegosouzapw/OmniRoute
## Quick take
A broad AI gateway focused on quota-aware routing, provider fallback, and token compression for coding-agent clients.
## Score
**84/100**
- **Harness relevance — 24/30:** Provides model routing, quota management, fallback, and compression around coding-agent traffic.
- **Practical pattern value — 23/25:** Quota-aware selection, last-known-good stickiness, fallback layers, and explicit routing strategies are reusable.
- **Evidence and maturity — 16/20:** The active MIT project documents releases, a large provider catalog, methods, examples, and ongoing audits.
- **Novelty — 13/15:** Combining free-tier accounting, many routing strategies, resilience, and token compression is distinctive.
- **Risk-adjusted accessibility — 8/10:** A zero-credential route lowers trial cost, but third-party quotas and a rapidly changing catalog add uncertainty.
## Why it matters
- Exposes numerous deterministic selection and resilience strategies behind one endpoint.
- Its free-tier pool accounting illustrates how to avoid double-counting shared quotas while routing across providers.
## Caveat
Provider availability, eligibility, quotas, and headline capacity can change independently of the code, and none of the routing claims were benchmarked here.
