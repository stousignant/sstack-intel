# Justin0504/Aegis
## Quick take
Aegis is a pre-execution policy and audit layer intended to govern agent tool calls before side effects.
## Score
**76/100**
- **Harness relevance — 20/30:** Pre-execution policy, approvals, audit, identity, and kill controls are a substantial agent-harness security component.
- **Practical pattern value — 22/25:** Tool-call interception, explainable policy decisions, approval queues, and audit sinks are directly reusable patterns.
- **Evidence and maturity — 14/20:** The active project has detailed documentation and MIT licensing, supporting an active documented-project anchor.
- **Novelty — 13/15:** The combined policy firewall, compliance cockpit, signed audit, and cross-agent detectors are distinctive.
- **Risk-adjusted accessibility — 7/10:** MIT licensing and quick deployment help, but a security gateway and dashboard create a broad trusted surface.
## Why it matters
- Pairs deterministic blocking and human approval with explainable policy matches and audit records.
- Extends governance across agent identity, memory-poisoning signals, SIEM sinks, and operational suspension.
