# greyhaven-ai/autocontext
## Quick take
A recursive evaluation harness that turns repeated agent runs into durable lessons, datasets, playbooks, and optional training artifacts.
## Score
**89/100**
- **Harness relevance — 30/30:** It is a full harness for iterative agent execution, evaluation, retained learning, and future-run improvement.
- **Practical pattern value — 23/25:** Durable outcomes, protected evaluation inputs, promotion gates, leases, and replayable artifacts are transferable.
- **Evidence and maturity — 16/20:** The active Apache-2.0 project has PyPI and npm packages, extensive docs, versioned changes, and operator surfaces.
- **Novelty — 15/15:** A recursive improvement loop retaining both useful lessons and negative outcomes is unusually differentiated.
- **Risk-adjusted accessibility — 5/10:** Quickstarts exist, but the Python and TypeScript control planes, providers, evaluators, and optional remote compute are complex.
## Why it matters
- Encodes promotion, regression, specialist, plateau, and negative-result evidence instead of keeping only apparent wins.
- Adds restart-safe campaign operations and capability-scoped local or remote execution around the improvement loop.
## Caveat
The broad, multi-runtime system has substantial operational surface, and no scenario, evaluator, or promotion behavior was exercised in this review.
